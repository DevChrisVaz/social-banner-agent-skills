# Visual QA Checklist

**Message:** clear in two seconds; one claim; evidence-backed benefits; real CTA.

**Composition:** one hero; clear reading path; sufficient copy-safe space; secondary footer.

**Legibility/accessibility:** readable at feed size; sufficient contrast; safe crop; alt-text available; no color-only meaning.

**Production:** correct ratio; no AI-garbled text/fake logos; verified claims/contact data; licensed assets; no compression artifacts.
