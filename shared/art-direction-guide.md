# Art Direction Guide

Default direction: clean futuristic corporate technology advertising, modern editorial layout, restrained 3D/isometric SaaS illustration, soft studio lighting, generous negative space, simple supporting modules, subtle gradients, and one protagonist.

Translate a service into a concrete metaphor: integration hub, modular app stack, secure portal, automation flow, product dashboard, or analytics command center. Avoid random circuitry, floating code, robot faces, dense glass UI, and unlicensed marks.

Generate background art only. Add final copy, logos, URLs, and exact icons as editable assets afterward.
