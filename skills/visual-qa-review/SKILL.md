# Visual QA Review

Evaluate the asset as an advertisement at feed size. Read `../../shared/visual-qa-checklist.md`.

1. Identify the two-second takeaway and score every checklist category Pass, Needs work, or Blocker.
2. Treat illegible CTA, incorrect claims, inaccessible text, fake logos, and AI-garbled type as Blockers.
3. Give the three highest-impact fixes in priority order with a concrete reason.
4. Keep brand-rule compliance separate: send that review to `brand-consistency-check`.
