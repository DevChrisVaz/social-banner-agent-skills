# Brand Consistency Check

Compare assets to the approved system, rather than applying generic taste. Read `../../shared/brand-system-template.md`.

1. List the explicit brand rules and inspect each asset against them.
2. Mark every item as Match, Drift, or Violation; distinguish intentional campaign variation from inconsistency.
3. Prioritize violations that affect recognition, trust, or accessibility.
4. Recommend the smallest change that brings the asset back into system.
